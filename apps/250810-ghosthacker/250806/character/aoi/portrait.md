# Portrait Prompt for Aoi

## Subject
- An 18-year-old rookie Ghost Hacker, recently arrived in Tokyo from the countryside. He is sincere, earnest, and a bit naive.

## Appearance
- **Face:** A youthful, friendly face with a look of genuine curiosity and openness. His expression is slightly wide-eyed, taking in the big city and the world of Ghost Hacking.
- **Hair:** A simple, slightly messy haircut.
- **Build:** Average build, looks like a typical teenager.

## Clothing & Style
- **Outfit:** Simple, practical, and casual clothes. A plain t-shirt or hoodie and jeans. He is more focused on function than fashion. He might have a new, modern backpack filled with gadgets.
- **Accessories:** A new-looking smartwatch or a pair of wireless earbuds.

## Setting & Lighting
- **Background:** A bustling street in Shibuya or Akihabara at dusk, with the neon lights of the city creating a colorful, slightly overwhelming backdrop. The background is blurred to keep the focus on him.
- **Lighting:** The complex, colorful lighting from the city's neon signs reflects on his face, highlighting his sense of wonder and perhaps a little bit of being lost in a new, exciting world.

## Composition & Mood
- **Shot:** Medium close-up. He is looking around with a sense of awe.
- **Mood:** Youthful, hopeful, and earnest. The portrait should capture his "newcomer" status and his pure-hearted enthusiasm for technology and his dream of becoming a great Hacker.
- **Quality:** Photorealistic, 8K. The image should have a cinematic feel, with a nice bokeh effect from the city lights.

## Chapter 0: Planning — "Know Thyself"

All hacking begins with a clear goal.
As ethical hackers, the first spirit we hack is not someone else.  The first thing we must do is take control of ourselves.

What is your operating system?  By what principles do you run?
If you feel the urge to hack, you are already under external control—culture, history, education, etc.

**Our objective is to escape the illusion of "self vs. other," to connect truly with others and with the web of causality itself.**
Ultimately we aim to rewrite our inner OS with our own hands and reach the state of **"enlightenment."**

The target is not an enemy.  It is the partner we must connect with.  Without this intention, hacking degenerates into mere violence.

When this hack is complete, you will have access to everything—and the power to generate.

## Chapter 1: Reconnaissance — "Observation"

All hacking starts with thorough information gathering.
And the very first target you must recon is always **yourself**.  Know thyself before you know the enemy—this is the iron law.

### Redefining the "Self" and Recon of the Network

In the first recon, many people make the mistake of limiting "themselves" to their body, experiences, or knowledge.
The pioneers first corrected that misunderstanding: **the individual self does not exist**—that is the starting line.

So what do you recon to know yourself?  Everything that is **strongly correlated** with you.
Parents, ancestors, siblings, friends, language, acquaintances—every single thing that has ever been linked to you as events.
The totality of those links **is** the system called "you," while **you** are merely the observer of those linked phenomena.

Therefore, what you must recon is **everything that influences you**.  Only from this perspective are you finally ready to recon the network that is yourself and your target.

### Passive Recon: Kernel Dump via Vipassanā Meditation

How well do you know the spec sheet of your own OS?  Have you ever run the `dmesg` command on your soul?

The first tool we use at this step is the meditation practiced by pioneers like **Dōgen**.  His quiet monitoring tool "shikantaza" observes one's own thought log just as it is.  In the same vein, **Vipassanā** meditation observes what is happening in one's mind and body without evaluation or judgment—simply "seeing" it.  This is equivalent to real-time dumping of your kernel messages.

Specifically, continuously record the following logs:

* **Trigger**: What event (input) …
* **Emotions & Thoughts**: What emotion (process) was launched and what thought (log) was output?
* **Bodily Sensations**: How did CPU usage (heart rate), temperature (body heat), breathing depth, and fine bodily sensations change?

By persisting in this logging, you will reveal your default configs—"common sense" and "values."  You will discover scripts that automatically spin up certain processes in response to certain inputs.  These are your vulnerabilities—and also your strongest weapons—in hacking others.

### Active Recon: Footprinting from Digital & Physical Exhaust

This is like gathering info from the target's exhaust, both digital and physical—still not sending direct packets (talking) yet.

* **Public Info (OSINT)**: Social media posts, clothing, speech, relationships.  The "Public API" a target exposes to the world offers massive information.  Who are they connected to, what do they like, what do they share?  All become clues to infer the target's config files.
* **Observation**: How do they behave in a group?  Whose words influence them?  Whom do they influence?  What expressions do they show?  What makes them laugh, what makes them frown?

This mirrors **Carl Jung's** word-association experiment: record what output word appears and how quickly when a stimulus word is given, revealing the hidden directory path to complexes in the unconscious.  We too infer internal structure from every output—speech, expression, social posts.

From the information gathered in this phase, build a provisional profile of the target.  What OS (values) are they running?  What applications (hobbies, work) do they run daily?  And what external networks (community, family, culture) influence that OS?  Form hypotheses.

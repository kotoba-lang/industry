## Chapter 6: Covering Tracks — "Wu-Wei-Ziran"

A skilled hacker erases traces at the end—but not to hide evidence.  **The final stage elevates "hacking" from technique to art.**

Through the previous phases you consciously used techniques—API calls in conversation, vulnerability scans, the shared experience of DRUG.  But at this stage, you erase even the consciousness of "using" techniques.

* API calls become natural curiosity.
* Vulnerability scans become pure interest.
* DRUG becomes heartfelt compassion.

The technical scaffolding that supported your relationships is removed, leaving only a beautiful, intuitive, authentic connection of souls.  Hacking turns into unconscious art.

The most crucial covering of tracks is **never abusing the root privilege you obtained**.  Even though you know the target's deepest vulnerabilities, you protect and respect them.  You never show or tamper with the secret log files you hold.  That perfect reliability melts your presence seamlessly into the system—the ultimate stealth.

Why must you not abuse it?  Because once you see the interaction as non-separate, causality returns to you.  In the process so far you gained the insight that self does not exist; old identities and perceptions may have collapsed, crashed, stopped.  Relax—it's normal.  You became root.  And the one you hacked is also yourself.

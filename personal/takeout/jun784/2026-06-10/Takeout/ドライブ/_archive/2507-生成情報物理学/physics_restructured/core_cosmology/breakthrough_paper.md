## Abstract

We present a revolutionary framework for cosmology based on **generative physics**—the principle that information processing and quantum computation are fundamental to cosmic evolution. Our approach treats the universe as an emergent computational system where classical space-time, quantum fields, and dark components arise from underlying information dynamics. This framework resolves major cosmological tensions (σ₈ and H₀ problems), predicts new physics signatures for axions, sterile neutrinos, and primordial black holes, and provides testable predictions for next-generation experiments. We demonstrate that universe evolution follows algorithmic principles with **information** as the fundamental physical quantity, suggesting reality itself is a computational process. Our results indicate that cosmological parameters emerge from quantum information processing rates, opening entirely new avenues for understanding the deepest questions in physics.

## Chapter 2: Scanning — "Discrimination"

Based on the profile obtained in recon, you finally initiate contact with the target.  You send the first packet and, from the system's responses, draw out more detailed information—this is the **Scanning** phase.

### Spirit as Information: Vectorizing the Mind

As argued in "IN — Information Is a Physical Quantity," information in physics is treated as a physical magnitude exchangeable with energy.  Applying this viewpoint, we quantify the target's mental state—**Spirit**—as information.

The fragments of information obtained through dialogue (words, facial expressions, tone of voice) are data points sampled from innumerable elements composing the target's state.  Collect these data points and represent them as vectors in a high-dimensional space, making the target's mental state **visible**.

* **State Space**: Define a multi-dimensional space whose axes are the target's values, beliefs, emotions, memories, etc.
* **Vectorization**: Using the information gathered through scanning, determine the target's coordinate on each axis and generate a state vector.  For example, learning that "family is important" raises the value on the "family" dimension.

Through this vectorization of spirit, we grasp the target's internal state not as an abstract impression but as a manipulable information model.  We can analyze which dimensions are most susceptible (vulnerabilities) and to which vector adding energy will shift the state most easily (persuadability).

The "passion," "desire," "fear," and "anxiety" unearthed in this scan phase are singularities in the vector space—the vulnerabilities to exploit in the next **Gaining Access** phase.

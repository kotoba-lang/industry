
# Scope and non-scope

## In scope

- L1 recalculation from current analysis-ready data where available.
- L2 reconstruction from the exact current provider/output checkpoint.
- L3 visual reassembly where only an aggregate raster checkpoint survives.
- Independent implementation of current artifact calculations, layouts, and
  machine-readable source tables.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Provider-level rerun from 41,528 individual control genotypes.
- Recovery of all-cohort case/control mapping, sex, PC1–PC10, or full-cohort
  individual PGS solely for this artifact-reconstruction task.
- Reproduction of Genequest-internal commands or environment.
- Reintroduction of removed PCA, signed-Z scatter, downsampling, obsolete
  Figure 2A, older 18-locus, or other stale submission artifacts.

The absence of an out-of-scope provider-level source is not a Phase 5C missing-
input failure when the exact current checkpoint is present.
